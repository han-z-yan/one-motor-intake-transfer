Speeds with a 6000 rpm motor: intake 1961 rpm in forward and 2000 rpm in reverse, transfer 2000 rpm in forward. All three belts fit their centre distance to within 0.01 mm, because the motor and shaft 1 were placed to suit them. The motor pulley has no outer flanges, so the 51T and 75T pulleys carry the flanges. The output belt runs under the lower geared roller's shaft and over the rear drive motor, with about 4.5 mm to each. The output pulley sits past the motor plate, about 2 mm from the motor can.
